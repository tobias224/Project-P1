\section{Introduction}
\label{introduktion}
According to a study conducted by team.blue called "The Digital Maturity Report", small and medium-sized organizations are reluctant to implement new it-solutions.\cite{smvdanmark} 
A small organization has between 10-49 employees and 10 million euros or less in turnover, whereas a medium sized-organization has 50-249 employees and 50 million euros or less in turnover \cite{e-conomic}. 
The report points out that the mentioned organizations hesitates to invest in it-solutions due to insecurity and lack of knowledge.\cite{smvdanmark}  

In order to make small and medium-sized organizations use new technology in the future, it is essential to make the technology easy to use and accessible.\cite{smvdanmark} \\ \\ 
An essential tool for organizations of all sizes are file sharing, both internally and externally. Common file sharing tools are Microsoft Sharepoint and Google Docs. Google Docs is an example of a cloud-based software as a service (SaaS). \cite{Pedersen} The mentioned cloud-based file sharing services are built on a role-based access control (RBAC), which means that permissions regarding rights to read and edit a file is given by predefined roles. \cite{RBAC_vs._ABAC}
An organization with less than 10 employees in a working group would profit from using role-based access control (ABAC), as it is easier to implement and use. \cite{RBAC_vs._ABAC} \\ \\
Identity and access management is crucial when it comes to secure data and file sharing within an organization. Many organizations gives too many employees access to different files without any considerations regarding access. \cite{sikkerdigital} Seeing how a role-based access control is easier for small and medium-sized organizations, it is the obvious choice of file sharing. However, an attribute-based access control is considered more secure, since it is less vulnerable to attacks due to features such as time restrictions and more nuanced access.\cite{RBAC_vs._ABAC} It increases the risk of valuable data being accessible for cyber criminals. \cite{sikkerdigital}

\section{Objective}
The objective of our project is to examine the current situation for small and medium-sized organizations regarding file sharing and to analyze the difference between role-based access control and attribute-based access control. \\

Our motivation stems from helping small and medium-sized organizations to prevent unauthorized access from a third party by securing their current method of file sharing. Further, the objective is to regulate access to data from external organizations to prevent unnecessary abuse or manipulating data. This should be done in a manner that is easy, accessible and affordable.\\
